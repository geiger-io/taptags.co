Getting started

  1. Open TapTags.
  2. Enter the phone number you want to share.
  3. Set a 4-digit lock code.
  4. Hold your phone near an NFC tag.
  5. Anyone who taps the tag can call you. They do not need TapTags installed.

You can update or reprogram a tag at any time using your lock code.

Compatible phones

iPhone: requires NFC hardware (iPhone 7 and later) and iOS 15.0 or later.

Android: requires a phone with NFC hardware and Android 7.0 or later.

Compatible tags

TapTags works with common writable NFC tags such as NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216. Tags must be writable (not locked) so the app can program them.

Does the person I share my tag with need the app?

No. Tapping a programmed tag opens the phone dialer with your number. The person tapping it does not need TapTags installed.

Can someone else change what my tag does?

Setting a 4-digit lock code when you program the tag reduces the chance that someone else can rewrite it. You can wipe or overwrite your own tag at any time using your code.

Does TapTags store my number on a server?

No. Your phone number and lock code are written to the physical NFC tag, not uploaded to a server. See the privacy policy for details.
